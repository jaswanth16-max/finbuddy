Built with clean Django fundamentals, FinBuddy strips away convoluted bank syncing errors and gives you pure, reliable personal finance power.

Complete Transactions

Easily record income and expense entries with titles, custom amounts, categories, and dates. Filter by month, search descriptions, and edit entries anytime.

Intelligent Budgets

Set spending limits by category for each month. FinBuddy calculates live expenses, shows visual progress bars, and alerts you when you're nearing or exceeding limits.

Plan for emergency funds, vacations, or major milestones. Track your saved balances, calculate completion percentages, and make quick deposits as you save.
